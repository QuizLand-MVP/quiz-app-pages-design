How to host a live quiz

Four quick steps

1

Choose your quiz

Pick a theme, difficulty, question count, and timer length. Shorter timers keep the pace quick; longer ones give younger or mixed-age groups more breathing room.

2

Share the code

Players scan the QR code or type the game code into their phone's browser. There's nothing to install, so everyone can join in seconds.

3

Race the timer

Once the host starts the round, every player answers on their own phone before the countdown hits zero. Faster correct answers earn bonus points.

4

Watch the leaderboard

Scores update on the shared screen after every question, so the whole room can see standings shift right up to the final round.

What you need

Simple setup

  • One laptop or TV for the shared quiz screen
  • Friends or family ready for a quiz night
  • A smartphone for each player
  • A stable internet connection
  • Around 10–20 minutes for one quiz
  • A little competitive spirit — and some luck

Tips for a better live quiz

Host tips

  • Mix quiz themes and question difficulty
  • Explain the quiz rules before you start
  • Keep quiz rounds short and moving
  • Choose trivia questions everyone can enjoy
  • Give players enough time to answer
  • Avoid long waits between quiz questions
  • Keep the live leaderboard visible
  • Mix easy questions with tougher challenges
  • Test your screen and phones before the quiz
  • Finish with a winner and offer a rematch

Hosting a live quiz remotely

Zoom, Teams, and video calls

You don't need everyone in the same room. Share your screen on the video call so remote players can see the questions and leaderboard, then send them the game code in the chat. Each player answers on their own phone or laptop browser, so the video call is only used for watching, not answering.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers

Do players need to download an app?

No. Players join from any phone browser using a game code or QR code — nothing to install.

How many players can join a live quiz?

Quiz Land games work for small groups and large ones alike, from a handful of friends to a full classroom or office team.

Is Quiz Land free to use?

Yes. You can start a game and host with players for free — just choose a theme and share the code.

Can I host a live quiz over Zoom or Teams?

Yes. Share your screen so remote players can see the questions and leaderboard, while each player answers from their own phone or laptop browser.

What devices work for hosting and playing?

Any device with a modern web browser works. Host from a laptop, TV browser, or shared screen, and players can join from a phone, tablet, or computer.

Can I pick my own quiz theme?

Yes. Choose from ready-made themes like movies and general knowledge, and set the difficulty, question count, and timer to match your group.
