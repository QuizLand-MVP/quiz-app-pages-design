Privacy Policy

Legal

Last updated: August 5, 2026

At present, the personal information Quiz Land collects is limited to your email address and the preferences associated with it. We collect this information when you sign in as a host using Google or a one-time code, subscribe through a newsletter form, or keep the newsletter option selected when submitting the host login form. We do not collect names, phone numbers, or payment details. Players who join a game do not create an account and are not asked to provide an email address.

What we collect

Quiz Land is a live quiz app for hosting games with family and friends. This page explains what information we collect from you and how we use it.

Why we collect it

A host's email address is used to authenticate the account, recognize the host as a returning user, determine the appropriate account role, deliver one-time codes and service-related notices, and permit the host to create and manage games. If you subscribe to marketing communications, we may also use that email address to send Quiz Land newsletters, product announcements, feature updates, offers, and related news. We do not sell email addresses or newsletter lists.

Newsletters and marketing communications

The host login page includes a newsletter checkbox beneath the email field. If that checkbox is selected when you continue, Quiz Land will record your newsletter preference and may use the email address submitted through the form to send marketing communications. Subscription to marketing communications is voluntary and is not a condition of creating or using a host account. You may clear the checkbox before continuing if you do not wish to subscribe.

You may withdraw consent, where consent is the applicable legal basis, or otherwise unsubscribe at any time by using the unsubscribe link included in a marketing email or by contacting support@quiz-land.com. We will process the request in accordance with applicable law. Any withdrawal of consent applies prospectively and does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal. After you unsubscribe, Quiz Land may retain the minimum information reasonably necessary to record and honor your request.

Unsubscribing from newsletters does not prevent Quiz Land from sending non-promotional communications that are reasonably necessary to operate, secure, or administer your account, including authentication codes, security notices, and material service or policy updates.

How it's stored

A host's email address and account role are stored in our database, associated with the account Supabase manages for you. Supabase, our authentication provider, may also retain that information as part of verifying your sign-in. Newsletter subscription preferences are stored with the relevant email address so that we can administer the subscription and honor unsubscribe requests. The app runs on Cloudflare, which processes requests to serve and secure the app but does not use your email for its own purposes. Authorized administrators may access or export newsletter records only as reasonably necessary to administer communications.

What your browser stores

Your browser locally stores a sign-in session token (managed by Supabase) and your language preference (English/Russian), so you don't have to sign in or reselect a language on every visit. Neither is shared with anyone else.

Cookies and tracking

We don't use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or third-party marketing pixels.

Your choices

You may ask us to delete your account, newsletter subscription, and/or email address at any time by contacting us below, subject to any information we are permitted or required to retain under applicable law. Signing out ends the current session but does not, by itself, delete stored account or subscription information.

As the app grows

As Quiz Land adds features like live multiplayer games, this policy will be updated first, and we'll only collect additional data described here.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at support@quiz-land.com.
