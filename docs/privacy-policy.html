Privacy Policy

Legal

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Right now, the only personal information we collect is your email address, either when you sign in as a host (with Google, or a one-time code we email you), or when you submit it on our coming-soon page to join the beta waitlist. We do not collect names, phone numbers, payment details, or any other personal data. Players who join a game don't create an account and are never asked for an email address.

What we collect

Quiz Land is a live quiz app for hosting games with family and friends. This page explains what information we collect from you and how we use it.

Why we collect it

A host's email address is used only to recognize you as a returning host, determine whether you're a host or an admin, and let you manage the games you create. A waitlist email is used only to let us notify you when beta access or launch is ready, that's the one thing you signed up for by submitting it. Neither is used for anything else, and we don't sell either to anyone.

How it's stored

A host's email address and account role are stored in our database, associated with the account Supabase manages for you, Supabase, our authentication provider, also retains it as part of verifying your sign-in. A waitlist email is stored in our database on its own, with no account or sign-in attached to it. The app runs on Cloudflare, which processes requests to serve and secure the app but doesn't use your email for its own purposes. Admins can export the waitlist as a CSV file to reach out about beta invites — that export never leaves the admin dashboard on its own.

What your browser stores

Your browser locally stores a sign-in session token (managed by Supabase) and your language preference (English/Russian), so you don't have to sign in or reselect a language on every visit. Neither is shared with anyone else.

Cookies and tracking

We don't use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or third-party marketing pixels.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your account, waitlist entry, and/or email address at any time by contacting us below. Signing out ends your current session but doesn't, by itself, delete stored data, email us if you'd like it removed.

As the app grows

As Quiz Land adds features like live multiplayer games, this policy will be updated first, and we'll only collect additional data described here.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at support@quiz-land.com.
